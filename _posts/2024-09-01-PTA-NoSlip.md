## Abstract
We perform an observational study of modified gravity considering a potential inflationary interpretation of pulsar timing arrays (PTA). We use a motivated model known as no slip in which the gravitational wave propagation is modified. Specifically, by using two different parametrizations for the model, we find the approximate transfer functions for tensor perturbations. In this way, we obtain the spectral energy density of gravitational waves and use NANOGrav and IPTA second data release to constrain parameters of the model. In parametrization I, ξ is degenerate with log 10 A and γ and in parametrization II, cM is also degenerate with both log 10 A and γ. For cM , we only get an upper bound on the parameter. Thus, it is difficult to constrain them with percent level accuracy with the current PTA data.
